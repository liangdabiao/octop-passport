# Seven Bridges — Application Record

Text-only application record for the Königsberg Seven Bridges puzzle
(`seven-bridges`): what the twelve levels are, how solvability is guaranteed,
and what the finale teaches. Cover images and store metadata live outside this
repository; this archive is the in-repo summary the reference documents link to.

## The game

An offline one-stroke (Euler trail) puzzle on the FoloToy AI Passport. Each
level is a hand-crafted multigraph: landmasses are circles, bridges are lines,
and the goal is to cross **every bridge exactly once**. The number inside each
landmass is its degree; odd degrees are shown in gold, because where the trail
can start and end is the heart of the puzzle.

## Level bank

| # | Name | Landmasses × bridges | Odd vertices | Teaches |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Triangle | 3 × 3 | 0 | Any start returns home |
| 2 | Square | 4 × 4 | 0 | Route order starts to matter |
| 3 | Diagonal | 4 × 5 | 2 | First odd vertices; the start matters |
| 4 | Bowtie | 5 × 6 | 0 | The junction must be visited twice |
| 5 | Three-petal | 7 × 9 | 0 | Out-and-back petals |
| 6 | Ri-shape | 6 × 7 | 2 | Plan before moving |
| 7 | Pentagram | 5 × 7 | 2 | Odd vertices are the endpoints |
| 8 | Twin Ferry | 6 × 8 | 0 | First parallel bridges |
| 9 | Five Stars | 5 × 10 | 0 | K5; crossings hold no land |
| 10 | Twin Towers | 6 × 12 | 0 | Densest regular level |
| 11 | The Eighth Bridge | 4 × 8 | 2 | Königsberg + one bridge = solvable |
| 12 | Königsberg | 4 × 7 | 4 | Unsolvable — Euler's proof, then demolish one bridge |

Every solvable level is verified by `tests/test_sb_level.c`: odd-degree count
∈ {0, 2} and an actual Euler trail found by `main/sb_euler.c` (iterative
Hierholzer with result verification). The finale is pinned by the same test:
**demolishing any one of the seven bridges leaves exactly two odd vertices,
so any demolition choice is solvable.** Level 11 differs from level 12 by
exactly one bridge (north bank–south bank), which is the story's setup.

## Interaction

Three keys: Up/Down cycle the candidate (start landmass, then bridges at the
current landmass), OK crosses/confirms, double-click OK undoes, long-press Up
requests a hint, long-press Down restarts the level, long-press OK returns to
the level grid (except in the stuck finale, where it opens Euler's story).
Stars: 3 = no undo no hint, 2 = undid, 1 = used a hint; progress, stars and
the demolition state persist in NVS (namespace `bridges`).

## Audio

Ten short square-wave RTTTL tones plus two background loops ("Waterside" for
menus, "Walking Bridges" for play), synthesized in `main/sb_sfx.c`. The player
has a single I2S writer; effects preempt the loop via a generation counter and
a 300 ms arbiter resumes it when idle. `rtttl_player.c` gained one upstream
extension for this: `rtttl_player_play_loop()`.

## Delivery notes

- Fonts: 412-codepoint subsets at 16/24/32 px from Noto Sans CJK SC (SIL OFL
  1.1), generated by `tools/seven-bridges/gen_font.py`, including ★☆.
- LVGL pool 96 KB, main-task stack 12288, Bluetooth disabled (~70 KB heap
  saved) — the family's on-device lessons, applied unchanged.
- Host tests: `test_sb_level` (data + solvability + finale property),
  `test_sb_euler` (solver behaviour, including multigraph and dead-state
  rejection), `test_sb_game` (state machine, stars, demolish flow),
  `test_sb_volume`, `test_sb_wrap`.
